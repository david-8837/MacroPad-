THE LITTLE DETAILS FAQ

A few things
worth knowing.

Good questions deserve simple answers.

01What is ZERO Deck Studio?

It’s a desktop app for assigning actions to a nine-key macropad and clickable rotary knob. You can organize your setup across four layers and upload it to a compatible device.

02Where can I download the app?

The Download for Windows button downloads the unsigned test installer. It has not been tested with physical hardware; Windows may show a warning.

03Which operating system is supported?

Windows is the primary desktop target. Some actions rely on the desktop app and Windows integrations; those integrations are still awaiting real-world testing.

04Can I change my shortcuts later?

Yes. You can edit your layers and assignments in the app, review your changes, and upload your setup again. Presets can also give you a starting point.

05Is the hardware ready to use?

The hardware and bundled firmware are still awaiting physical testing. The V2 RGB power design is not approved; leave LED power and data disconnected until it is.

06What’s the difference between V1 and V2?

V1 is the keys-and-knob build without a screen or external LEDs. V2 adds a screen and planned RGB lighting. Both use a Waveshare RP2040-Zero, nine switches, and a clickable rotary encoder.
