FROM PARTS TO SHORTCUTS THE BUILD GUIDE

Make it.
Set it up.

A quick overview of the build, firmware, and desktop setup. Take your time and check the complete wiring guide before connecting power.

01 THE CASE

Choose a case.

Pick a case model from the library above when files are available. Print it and check the fit of your switches, board, and knob before final assembly.

02 THE WIRING

Wire the deck.

With power disconnected, follow the diagram for your build. Check the board silkscreen, component terminals, and continuity before connecting USB.

SEE THE DIAGRAMS ↘
03 THE SOFTWARE

Get Deck Studio.

The Windows desktop app is used for setup and flashing. An installer has not been released yet; check the releases page for updates.

WINDOWS RELEASES ↗
04 THE FIRMWARE

Flash the firmware.

Unplug USB, hold the RP2040-Zero’s BOOT button while reconnecting, then release. In the desktop app, open RP2040 Firmware & Bootloader, select Keys & Knob for V1 or Full Deck for V2, review the warning, and flash. Afterward, verify the board reconnects.

05 YOUR SHORTCUTS

Make it yours.

Once the compatible pad is connected, assign keys and the knob across your layers. Review your setup in Upload to deck, then upload all layers.

Before you begin. The hardware and both firmware images have not been physically tested. Verify your board and wiring before flashing. For V2, the RGB power design is not approved; leave LED power and data disconnected. The PDF also depicts an unapproved RGB circuit: low brightness does not make it safe. The website itself cannot flash the device.
